## 1. Purpose

PCAMG-0001 establishes the founding doctrine for transforming UCOS Ω∞ from a **Constitutional Hybrid
Governance** architecture into a **Principle-Centric Adaptive Meta-Governance** civilization architecture.

Its purpose is to define — as a governed, ratifiable proposal — a governance model in which:

1. **Invariant principles**, not documents or authorities, are the ultimate source of legitimacy.
2. **Governance itself is governed** (meta-governance) rather than assumed or hand-authored.
3. Governance systems are **generated from principles**, validated, simulated, evolved, and retired — never
   manually privileged.
4. Authority is **polycentric** — distributed across self-governing, principle-bound domains — with no
   domain holding absolute power.
5. Every artifact proves **Principle → Constitutional → Governance → Operational** compliance before
   activation.

PCAMG does not discard the existing UCOS governance corpus. It **reframes and subsumes** it: the ratified
Authority Layer (`AUTH-001..012`), the Constitution (`UCOS-CONST-001`), the Foundation Permanence invariants
(INV-1..13), and the canonical platform invariants (`INV-CORE-001`) are recognized as **already principle-
derived**, and PCAMG makes that derivation explicit, registry-driven, and evolvable.

only upon Authority Board enrollment.

```
Layer 0  Invariant Principles          (PCAMG-0002 — Universal Principle Registry)
Layer 1  Meta-Constitution             (PCAMG-0003)
Layer 2  Governance Generation         (PCAMG-0004)
Layer 3  Polycentric Governance Network(PCAMG-0005)

```

**Harmonization rule (proposed).** The PCAMG order does not *invert* the ratified hierarchy; it **re-roots**
it. The ratified `AUTHORITY` tier maps to PCAMG Layers 0–1 (principles + meta-constitution), the ratified
`CONSTITUTION` maps to Layer 1/Layer 4 domain constitutions, and everything from `ARCHITECTURE` downward maps
to Layers 5–8. Nothing may invert this order once enrolled (Article XI parallel).

## 4. Governance Model

PCAMG governs UCOS through five doctrinal commitments:

1. **Principle Supremacy.** No governance artifact — including constitutions — may violate a registered
   invariant principle (`PCAMG-0002`). Principles are immutable; governance is evolvable.
2. **Meta-Governance.** How constitutions and governance models are created, evolved, validated, and retired
   is itself governed by the Meta-Constitution (`PCAMG-0003`).
3. **Generation over Privilege.** Governance structures are *generated* from principles + meta-constitution
   (`PCAMG-0004`); none is manually privileged or hardcoded (IP-01, IP-02).
4. **Polycentrism.** Authority is distributed across self-governing, constitution-bound, principle-bound
   domains (`PCAMG-0005`); no domain obtains absolute authority; escalation terminates at the Authority
   Board (AUTH-009).
5. **Proof-Gated Activation.** No artifact is deployable without a Principle Compliance proof
   (`PCAMG-0007`), preserving Article IX gating and non-waivable S1/S3/S4.

## 5. Evolution Model

- **Principles immutable; governance evolvable** (`PCAMG-0006`). Amendment of a principle is a
  Constitutional-Majority Approval-Required Operation (AUTH-009 / AUTH-012); evolution of a governance model
  is a governed, migration-only (IP-14), backward-compatible (IP-15) act simulated and validated before
  application.
- All evolution is **append-only** (INV-10): superseded governance is retired with supersession links, never
  deleted.
- Governance performance is measured, failures detected, improvements proposed, changes simulated, and
  constitutional compatibility validated before any migration (`PCAMG-0006`).

## 7. Compliance Requirements

If (and only if) PCAMG is enrolled, every future UCOS subsystem must demonstrate, before activation:

```
Principle Compliance  →  Constitutional Compliance  →  Governance Compliance  →  Operational Compliance
```

- **Principle Compliance** — no violation of any registered invariant principle (`PCAMG-0002`).
- **Constitutional Compliance** — conformance to the Meta-Constitution and applicable domain constitutions.
- **Governance Compliance** — generation/validation through the Governance Generation Framework
  (`PCAMG-0004`) and the owning domain (`PCAMG-0005`).
- **Operational Compliance** — gate conformance (Article IX; `GATE-*`) and non-waivable S1/S3/S4.

No PCAMG-governed artifact may exist outside the PCAMG hierarchy once enrolled; until then, the ratified
hierarchy governs.
