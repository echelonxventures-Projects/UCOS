## 1. Purpose

GD-0001 is the founding doctrine of the UCOS Ω∞ Constitutional Refoundation Program. It establishes, as a
governed and ratifiable proposal, **Principle-Centric Adaptive Meta-Governance (PCAMG)** as the supreme
governance doctrine of UCOS: a civilization-scale, registry-driven, deterministic, polycentric governance
architecture in which **invariant principles — not documents, organizations, executions, or artificial
intelligences — are the sole origin of sovereignty and legitimacy.**

The doctrine answers one question at the highest level: *from what does UCOS governance derive its authority,
and how does that authority flow?* Its answer is codified in the Sovereignty Origin Doctrine (`GD-0002`),
made concrete in the Invariant Principles Registry (`PCAMG-0000`), and operationalized by the PCAMG framework
series (`PCAMG-1000..8000`).

## 3. Doctrinal Position

UCOS today is a **Constitutional Hybrid Governance** architecture: a ratified Authority Layer
(`AUTH-001..012`), a Constitution (`UCOS-CONST-001`), foundation-permanence invariants (INV-1..13), and
canonical integrity invariants (`INV-CORE-001`). GD-0001 does not discard this corpus. It **re-roots** it,
asserting that all of it is *already principle-derived* and making that derivation explicit, registry-driven,
deterministic, and evolvable.

PCAMG is declared **supreme** in the precise sense that, on enrollment, **no governance artifact — including
constitutions — may violate a registered invariant principle**, and no authority may exist that is not derived
through the governed authority flow. Supremacy is a property of the *principles*, not of any office, model,
or machine.

## 4. Authority Flow (invariant)

The doctrine fixes a single, non-invertible authority flow:

```
Sovereignty Origin (Invariant Principles)          [GD-0002]
        ↓
Invariant Principles Registry                       [PCAMG-0000]
        ↓
Meta-Constitution (governs governance)              [PCAMG-1000]
        ↓
Governance Generation (principles → governance)     [PCAMG-2000]
        ↓
Polycentric Governance Network                      [PCAMG-3000]
        ↓
Federated Domain Governance                         [PCAMG-4000]
        ↓
Organizations
        ↓
Implementations
        ↓
Executions                                          [PCAMG-5000]
```

**Never the reverse.** No lower tier may confer authority on, override, amend, or reinterpret a higher tier.
Executions derive no sovereignty; organizations derive no sovereignty; artificial intelligences derive no
sovereignty. Authority flows only downward from the invariant principles; legitimacy claims flow only upward
for verification (`SPEC-TRACEABILITY-FRAMEWORK`).
