## 1. Overview
Sequences the Ω∞ ambition into program increments **PI-2 through PI-14**, each gated on prior governance and on
the current PI-1 foundation. The roadmap is deliberately **conservative and reality-anchored** (`UCOS-UEA-0012`):
existential increments are late and conditional; near-term increments simply complete the *planetary* platform.

## 5. Sequencing Rules
- **Planetary before existential.** PI-2..PI-6 complete the current platform *before* any existential increment.
- **Amendment-gated.** PI-10..PI-14 cannot begin unless their INV dependency is *enrolled* (Board act) — not
  merely proposed.
- **Lock-gated.** No PI begins before Article IX release (P-1).
- **Append-only.** Every increment extends; none redesigns the foundation (INV-10/INV-13).
