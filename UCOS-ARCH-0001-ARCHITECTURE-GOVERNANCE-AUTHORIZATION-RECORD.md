## 1. Constitutional Admissibility Assessment  *(Section A — Is Architecture Governance constitutionally admissible?)*

The doctrinal shape the constitution admits — enumerated across the ratified corpus and enforced on the
entire governance runtime — is: **read-only in assessment · append-only in evidence · deterministic ·
fail-closed · propose-only · originates no authority · executes nothing.** A candidate governance
function is admissible **if and only if** its *defining act* conforms to that shape.

Testing the defining act of Architecture Governance — *to classify, trace, measure coverage, and attest
integrity* — against the shape:

| Act of Architecture Governance | Shape it takes | Admissible? |
|---|---|---|
| Classify each capability | Read constitution + inventory → attest a classification | **YES** — read-and-attest, no mutation |
| Assign owner / program / track | Record a governance assignment (append-only) | **YES** — governance record, not an executed effect |
| Trace capability → Mission…Deliverables | Derive and attest a traceability relation | **YES** — pure derivation over ratified inputs |
| Measure coverage / completeness | Compute a verdict over the corpus | **YES** — deterministic predicate, fail-closed |
| Detect / flag drift | Compare state to constitutional intent → raise finding | **YES** — attestation; it *reports*, it does not *correct by force* |

Every defining act is a **reasoning-and-attestation act**. None requires an ACTIVE state, an activation
pathway, an effectful mutation, or the origination of authority. This is the *same admissible shape*
already ratified for the entire Constitutional Governance Runtime — the difference is only the object of
reasoning (here: architecture and capability coverage, rather than authority or eligibility).

**The decisive contrast with a denied layer.** A Governance *Execution* Layer was denied
(`PCAMG-RUNTIME-0301`) because its defining act — *to realize an outcome* — **is** the forbidden act
(execution / activation / mutation). Architecture Governance is the constitutional opposite: strip it of
any hypothetical enforcement power and what remains — classification, traceability, coverage,
attestation — **is the whole program**, and that residue is fully invariant-conformant. There is no
forbidden core to remove. The program is admissible without contradiction.

Moreover, admissibility here is *reinforced* by ratified doctrine rather than merely tolerated by it:
**P5 (Traceability End-to-End)**, **IP-08 (Traceability First, non-waivable)**, and **IP-10
(Auditability by Default, non-waivable)** are already-ratified constitutional obligations. Architecture
Governance is the standing mechanism by which those obligations are discharged during implementation.

**Determination: `CONSTITUTIONAL_ADMISSIBILITY_CONFIRMED`** — the defining act of Architecture
Governance is read-and-attest, fully conformant to the ratified governance shape, and directly serves
non-waivable constitutional obligations (IP-08, IP-10, P5).

## 3. Authority Assessment  *(Section C — Does Architecture Governance introduce any new authority source?)*

This is the pivotal question, and the answer is **no**.

Architecture Governance exercises exactly one kind of power: the power to **attest** — to classify, to
trace, to measure, and to raise findings about conformance. Attestation is **not** authority origination
(INV-9): it manufactures no sovereign or execution power; it merely reports the state of conformance
against authority that *already exists upstream* in the Invariant Principles, Meta-Constitution, and
Governance Generation.

Two failure modes are excluded:

1. **The program originates authority.** Excluded. Its verdicts bind nothing by their own force; they
   are governance records subordinate to the constitution. A finding of "drift" or "coverage gap"
   asserts a *fact about conformance*, not a new command. It cannot self-authorize, self-expand, or
   generate constitutional legitimacy.
2. **The program becomes a de-facto authority by enforcement.** Excluded **by the bound imposed in this
   record**: Architecture Governance is authorized as **propose-only**. It may *report, gate-by-
   attestation, and withhold a conformance verdict* (fail-closed), but it may not *itself* mutate,
   construct, or execute any corrective action. Correction remains an implementation act performed under
   authority originated upstream — never by the governance program acting on the world.

Because its only output is a subordinate attestation traceable to pre-existing constitutional authority,
Architecture Governance introduces **no new authority source**.

**Determination: `NO_NEW_AUTHORITY_INTRODUCED`** — the program is attestation-only and authority-
derivative; INV-9 is preserved.

## 4. Traceability Assessment  *(Section D — Can it guarantee traceability Mission → Vision → Requirements → Capabilities → Deliverables → Implementations?)*

Traceability is a **derivation over already-ratified, append-only artifacts** — precisely the class of
act the governance shape admits and executes deterministically. The repository already demonstrates the
substrate exists in fragmentary form (`UCOS-CAPABILITY-INVENTORY`, `UCOS-COVERAGE-MATRIX`,
`UCOS-CAPABILITY-DEPENDENCY-GRAPH`), proving the derivation is *feasible*; what is absent is a **permanent
program** that makes it *continuous, complete, and fail-closed*.

Under fail-closed discipline (INV-6), the traceability *guarantee* is well-formed:

- For each capability, the program requires a proven, source-traced chain
  `Mission → Vision → Goals → Values → Requirements → Constitutional Laws → Deliverables →
  Implementations`.
- Any capability whose chain is incomplete or unproven resolves to the **negative** verdict
  (`NOT_TRACEABLE` / `UNPROVEN`) — never an optimistic pass. Incompleteness is surfaced, not absorbed.

Thus the program cannot *fabricate* traceability, but it can and must *guarantee* one of two honest
outcomes for every capability: **traceable-and-proven**, or **flagged-as-untraceable**. That guarantee —
completeness of the *verdict*, fail-closed on the *gap* — is exactly what P5 and IP-08 (non-waivable)
require and what the program is fit to deliver.

**Determination: `TRACEABILITY_GUARANTEE_ADMISSIBLE`** — the program can guarantee that every capability
is either proven-traceable or explicitly flagged, satisfying P5 / IP-08 under fail-closed discipline.

## 5. Coverage Assessment  *(Section E — Can it guarantee complete constitutional coverage?)*

Coverage is a **predicate over the closed set of constitutional capabilities**: for the enumerated
constitution (Mission, Vision, Goals, Values, Laws, Layers 00–19, Cross-Cutting / Execution /
Infrastructure Fabrics, Platform Synthesis, Generated Platform Classes), does every element map to a
classified, owned, program-assigned, track-assigned capability?

Two properties make the coverage *guarantee* admissible:

1. **The constitution is a finite, ratified, enumerable set** — coverage is therefore *decidable* over
   it, not open-ended.
2. **Fail-closed on the residue** — any constitutional element without a complete mapping is reported as
   an **uncovered gap** (as `ARCH-GAP-001` / `UCOS-CAPABILITY-GAP-REPORT` already do for a snapshot),
   never silently omitted.

The program therefore guarantees a *measurable* coverage state at all times: a proven-covered set plus
an explicit uncovered-gap set whose union is the whole constitution. It cannot *manufacture* coverage of
an unimplemented capability — but making the gap **visible, measurable, and non-silent** is the coverage
guarantee the mission asks for (Objective 6), and it is admissible.

**Determination: `COVERAGE_GUARANTEE_ADMISSIBLE`** — complete, measurable coverage of the finite
constitutional set is guaranteeable, with all gaps surfaced fail-closed.

## 6. Architectural Integrity Assessment  *(Section F — Can it prevent architectural drift?)*

"Prevent" must be read honestly under fail-closed doctrine, and the honest reading is *containable*, not
*coercive*:

- Architecture Governance **prevents drift by attestation and gating**: it continuously compares
  implementation state to constitutional intent, and *withholds* a conformance verdict (fail-closed)
  wherever drift, duplication, or divergence is detected. An implementation that cannot obtain a
  positive conformance attestation is thereby *blocked from claiming constitutional standing* — the same
  mechanism by which every ratified layer gates advancement.
- It does **not** prevent drift by *force* — it does not itself rewrite, delete, or execute corrective
  changes on the codebase (that would be an execution act, forbidden). Prevention is structural
  (no-conformance-without-proof), not physical.

This is the strongest form of drift prevention available to a governance program without breaching the
invariant firewall, and it is sufficient: under IP-10 (Auditability, non-waivable) and the fail-closed
invariant (INV-6), an architecture change that cannot be traced and covered simply cannot be certified
as constitutional. Drift is thereby denied *standing*, which is the constitutional lever.

**Determination: `INTEGRITY_PREVENTION_ADMISSIBLE`** — the program prevents drift by fail-closed
attestation and gating (denial of constitutional standing to unproven change), without any forbidden
execution act.
