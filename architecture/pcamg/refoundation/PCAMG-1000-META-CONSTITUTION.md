## 1. Purpose

PCAMG-1000 is the **Meta-Constitution**: the constitution *of constitutions*. Where an ordinary constitution
governs a system, the Meta-Constitution governs **how any governance system (including constitutions) may be
created, derived, validated, interpreted, evolved, and retired**. It is subordinate only to the Sovereignty
Origin Doctrine (`GD-0002`) and the Invariant Principles Registry (`PCAMG-0000`).

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** the articles governing derivation of authority, principle supremacy, generation, polycentrism,
federation, evolution, interpretation, conflict resolution, legitimacy, amendment, and compliance — at the
meta level.

**Out of scope:** operational governance content (owned by generated governance systems and domain
constitutions); enrollment; any change to ratified artifacts; any release of Article IX.

## 3. Articles

### Article M-I — Principle Supremacy
No governance artifact — including this Meta-Constitution and any domain constitution — may violate a
registered invariant principle (`PCAMG-0000`). On conflict, the principle prevails; the artifact is rejected
or escalated. This article is itself bounded by the principles and may never be amended to weaken them.

### Article M-II — Derivation of Authority (void-without-derivation)
All authority is *derived*, never inherent (`GD-0002` S-III). An authority claim that cannot be traced upward
to a registered invariant principle through a continuous derivation chain (`SPEC-TRACEABILITY-FRAMEWORK`) is
**void**. No office, document, execution, AI, or organization holds authority except by verified derivation.

### Article M-III — No Sovereignty From Execution, AI, or Organizations
The three exclusions of `GD-0002` (S-IV/S-V/S-VI) are constitutional law at the meta level and are
non-waivable. No generated governance system, domain constitution, evolution, or interpretation may weaken or
invert them.

### Article M-IV — Governance Is Generated, Not Privileged
Governance systems are **generated** from principles and this Meta-Constitution via the Governance Generation
Framework (`PCAMG-2000`). No governance structure may be manually privileged, hardcoded, or self-declared
(IP-01/IP-02). Every governance system carries a generation record proving its derivation.

### Article M-V — Polycentric Authority
Authority is distributed across self-governing, principle-bound governance centers (`PCAMG-3000`) and
federated domains (`PCAMG-4000`). No center or domain holds absolute authority. Terminal escalation for
human-sovereignty matters is the Authority Board (AUTH-009), itself a delegate of PRIN-001 (`GD-0002` S-VI).

### Article M-VI — Determinism & Non-Ambiguity
Every governance rule has exactly one deterministic interpretation (`PCAMG-6000`). Given the principles, the
governance registry, and the inputs, a governance decision is reproducible (INV-6). Ambiguity is a defect that
is resolved by the Interpretation Framework, never by discretion.

### Article M-VII — Conflict Resolution
Conflicts between governance artifacts are resolved by the deterministic ordering of `PCAMG-7000`. In all
cases the higher-derivation artifact prevails, and the invariant principles prevail over everything. No
conflict is resolved by unaudited discretion.

### Article M-VIII — Amendment
- **Principles** (`PCAMG-0000`): amendable only by Constitutional Majority + AUTH-012, in the
  strengthen-only direction where declared. Never mutated by evolution (PRIN-014).
- **Meta-Constitution** (this artifact): amendable only by Constitutional Majority + AUTH-012, and never in a
  direction that violates Articles M-I / M-II / M-III.
- **Generated governance / domain constitutions / policies**: evolvable via the Adaptive Evolution Framework
  (`PCAMG-2000`/evolution), migration-only (IP-14), backward-compatible (IP-15), simulate-before-apply.
- All amendment is append-only (INV-10); superseded text is retired with supersession links, never deleted.

### Article M-IX — Legitimacy & Consent
Governance binds a party only where its legitimacy is established and, for rights-affecting action, the
party's informed, revocable consent is present (`PCAMG-8000`; PRIN-003/PRIN-007). Illegitimate governance is
void.

### Article M-X — Compliance-Gated Activation
No governance artifact or execution is active without a passing four-stage compliance proof —
Principle → Constitutional → Governance → Operational (`SPEC-CONSTITUTIONAL-COMPLIANCE-FRAMEWORK`). Non-waivable
S1/S3/S4 and the Article IX generation lock are preserved as operational gates.

### Article M-XI — Auditability & Traceability
Every governed action emits an immutable, attributable, independently-verifiable audit record (PRIN-006,
IP-10, `INV-CORE-02`), and every artifact carries a complete derivation trace (PRIN-004, IP-08). Governance
that cannot be reconstructed or traced is void.

### Article M-XII — Reconciliation With the Ratified Corpus
This Meta-Constitution reframes `UCOS-CONST-001` as a **domain/meta constitution derived from Layer-0
principles**. It does not delete it. Until the Authority Board reconciles the two via AUTH-012 (version
increment, append-only), `UCOS-CONST-001` remains in force and prevails on any conflict (`GD-0001` §6).

## 4. Governance Lifecycle Governed by the Meta-Constitution

```
CREATE      generate a governance system from principles + meta-constitution   (PCAMG-2000)
   ↓
VALIDATE    prove principle + constitutional conformance                       (SPEC-CONSTITUTIONAL-VALIDATION-RULES)
   ↓
INTERPRET   resolve to a single deterministic meaning                          (PCAMG-6000)
   ↓
ACTIVATE    only after the four-stage compliance proof passes                  (M-X)
   ↓
EVOLVE      migration-only, simulated, backward-compatible                      (PCAMG-2000 / evolution)
   ↓
RETIRE      append-only supersession; never deletion                           (INV-10, M-VIII)
```
